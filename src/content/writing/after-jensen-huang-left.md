<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">11 月 12 日，NVIDIA 發布公告。</span><span lang="en">On November 12, NVIDIA made an announcement.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">黃仁勳辭去總裁、執行長與董事職務，即刻生效。</span><span lang="en">Jensen Huang resigned as President, CEO, and board member, effective immediately.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">財務長 Colette Kress 暫代執行長，前 Synopsys 執行長 Harvey Jones 成為公司第一位董事長。</span><span lang="en">CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO, and former Synopsys CEO Harvey Jones became the company’s first-ever chair of the board.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">公告沒有寫原因。</span><span lang="en">The announcement gave no reason.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">NVIDIA 總部在加州聖塔克拉拉，離我念書的地方很近。</span><span lang="en">NVIDIA’s headquarters is in Santa Clara, California, very close to where I go to school.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">那天，有人在總部招牌下放了花和白蠟燭。</span><span lang="en">That day, someone left flowers and a white candle beneath the sign at headquarters.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">先說清楚，我不懂董事會裡發生了什麼。</span><span lang="en">Let me be clear first. I don’t know what happened inside the boardroom.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">媒體報導，這場風暴和他與一名涉嫌替中國工作的女子之間的關係有關，另外還有婚外情的爭議。</span><span lang="en">Media reports say the storm is tied to his relationship with a woman suspected of working for China, along with controversy over extramarital affairs.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">董事會啟動了調查，也聲明沒有理由相信公司機密或國家安全外洩，辭職不代表指控成立。</span><span lang="en">The board launched an investigation and stated there is no reason to believe company secrets or national security were compromised, and that his resignation does not mean the allegations are proven.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">國會要他出面說明。</span><span lang="en">Congress has asked him to come forward and explain.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我只看得到新聞，看不到背後。</span><span lang="en">I can only see the news. I can’t see what is behind it.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我只知道，凡是和政治有關的事，都比標題複雜。</span><span lang="en">All I know is that anything involving politics is more complicated than the headline.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">所以這篇我不判斷誰對誰錯。</span><span lang="en">So in this piece I won’t judge who is right or wrong.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我只寫這件事對我意味著什麼。</span><span lang="en">I’m only writing about what this means to me.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他是從哪裡來的</span><span lang="en">Where He Came From</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">黃仁勳 1963 年出生在台南。</span><span lang="en">Jensen Huang was born in Tainan in 1963.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">小時候在泰國住過，9 歲被送到美國。</span><span lang="en">He lived in Thailand as a child and was sent to the United States at age 9.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">青少年時期，他在 Denny’s 洗碗、收桌子。</span><span lang="en">As a teenager, he washed dishes and bussed tables at Denny’s.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">1993 年，他和兩位朋友在 Denny’s 的座位上創辦 NVIDIA。</span><span lang="en">In 1993, he and two friends founded NVIDIA at a table in Denny’s.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">公司曾經差一點倒閉。</span><span lang="en">The company once came close to collapse.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他飛到日本，請 Sega 繼續出資，那筆 500 萬美元讓公司撐了下去。</span><span lang="en">He flew to Japan and asked Sega to keep funding him, and that $5 million kept the company alive.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">之後是 33 年的時間，從遊戲顯示卡一路做到 AI 算力。</span><span lang="en">What followed was 33 years, from gaming graphics cards all the way to AI compute.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">台灣和泰國</span><span lang="en">Taiwan and Thailand</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我在美國將近 8 年。</span><span lang="en">I have been in the United States for almost 8 years.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">這 8 年裡，我每次介紹自己是台灣人，都有人問：是泰國嗎？</span><span lang="en">In those 8 years, every time I said I was from Taiwan, someone asked: Thailand?</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">Taiwan 和 Thailand，在很多人耳裡差不多。</span><span lang="en">To many people’s ears, Taiwan and Thailand sound about the same.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">這幾年變了。</span><span lang="en">The last few years changed that.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">大家開始用 AI。</span><span lang="en">Everyone started using AI.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">AI 要訓練，訓練要算力，算力要 GPU。</span><span lang="en">AI needs training, training needs compute, compute needs GPUs.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">往下追，會追到 NVIDIA，再追到台積電。</span><span lang="en">Follow the chain down and you reach NVIDIA, then TSMC.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">很多人沒讀過任何晶片的資料，卻因為 ChatGPT 第一次認真聽到「台灣」兩個字。</span><span lang="en">Many people have never read a word about chips, yet because of ChatGPT they heard the word “Taiwan” seriously for the first time.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">黃仁勳這幾年在 GTC 和各種公開場合，一次又一次提到台灣。</span><span lang="en">In recent years, at GTC and many other public appearances, Jensen Huang has mentioned Taiwan again and again.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他出生在這裡，他愛這裡，你聽得出來。</span><span lang="en">He was born here, he loves this place, and you can hear it.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">對我這個 24 歲的留學生來說，他是我在美國的標竿。</span><span lang="en">For me, a 24-year-old international student, he is my benchmark in the United States.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">每次在 LinkedIn 上看到「Denny’s 洗碗工」那一行，我就覺得自己可以再撐一下。</span><span lang="en">Every time I see the “Denny’s dishwasher” line on LinkedIn, I feel I can hold on a little longer.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">一個朋友的比喻</span><span lang="en">A Friend’s Metaphor</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我有個創業的朋友 Sean。</span><span lang="en">I have a friend, Sean, who is a founder.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他前陣子跟我說，他想卸下 CEO 的位置。</span><span lang="en">Not long ago he told me he wants to step down as CEO.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他的形容是：像把自己養大的孩子，交給別人領養。</span><span lang="en">His description: it feels like giving the child you raised to someone else to adopt.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">你明明知道孩子是你的，但你覺得別人比你更適合帶他走下一段。</span><span lang="en">You know the child is yours, but you believe someone else is better suited to lead him through the next stretch.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">Sean 是自己選的。</span><span lang="en">Sean is choosing this himself.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">黃仁勳的情況完全不同，外界到現在都還在拼湊細節。</span><span lang="en">Jensen Huang’s situation is completely different, and the outside world is still piecing together the details.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">但兩件事的結果一樣，CEO 換人了。</span><span lang="en">But the outcome of both is the same: the CEO changes.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">而我發現，我對 Sean 的難過少一點，對黃仁勳的難過多很多。</span><span lang="en">And I noticed I feel less sad about Sean, and much more sad about Jensen Huang.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">因為我只能遠遠看著這個人，卻從來沒機會跟他說謝謝。</span><span lang="en">Because I can only watch this man from far away, and I never had a chance to say thank you to him.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我在學的那件事</span><span lang="en">What I’m Learning</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">這個學期，我在打基礎。</span><span lang="en">This semester, I’m building foundations.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我 vibe code 了近兩年。</span><span lang="en">I have vibe coded for almost two years.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">需求丟給 AI，程式吐出來，跑起來，很爽。</span><span lang="en">I hand the request to AI, the code comes out, it runs, and it feels great.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">很多東西一次就成功，而且以後只會更容易。</span><span lang="en">Many things work on the first try, and it will only get easier.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">越來越多不是工程師的人，會用這種方式做事。</span><span lang="en">More and more people who are not engineers will work this way.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">但我開始卡住了。</span><span lang="en">But I started getting stuck.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">程式出錯時，我把整段錯誤訊息貼回去，請 AI 修。</span><span lang="en">When the code threw an error, I pasted the whole error message back and asked AI to fix it.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">它修好一個，冒出另一個。</span><span lang="en">It fixed one, and another popped up.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我說不出它改了哪一行，也說不出為什麼之前會壞。</span><span lang="en">I couldn’t say which line it changed, or why it had broken before.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">程式跑得出來，我卻解釋不了它為什麼跑得出來。</span><span lang="en">The code ran, but I couldn’t explain why it ran.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">所以我開始學 Python，一行一行讀。</span><span lang="en">So I started learning Python, reading it one line at a time.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">很慢，而且有時候很挫折。</span><span lang="en">It is slow, and sometimes frustrating.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">身邊有人覺得，既然 AI 會寫，你何必學。</span><span lang="en">People around me think: since AI can write it, why learn it?</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我懂這個邏輯。</span><span lang="en">I understand that logic.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我想要的是，它壞了的時候，我知道從哪裡開始修。</span><span lang="en">What I want is to know where to start fixing it when it breaks.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">基礎是層層相扣的。</span><span lang="en">Foundations interlock, layer on layer.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">你為了快跳過一層，那一層的問題不會消失，它只是排在後面等你。</span><span lang="en">If you skip a layer to go faster, that layer’s problems don’t disappear. They just line up behind you and wait.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">33 年</span><span lang="en">33 Years</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我現在比以前更懂黃仁勳那 33 年。</span><span lang="en">I understand Jensen Huang’s 33 years better now than before.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">一個人認定一件事，周圍的人覺得你傻，你還是做。</span><span lang="en">A person decides on something, the people around you think you’re foolish, and you keep doing it.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">靠的是對自己手上這件事的信任。</span><span lang="en">What carries you is trust in the thing in your own hands.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他靠這個把一家快倒的公司，做成全世界最重要的公司之一。</span><span lang="en">With that, he turned a nearly bankrupt company into one of the most important companies in the world.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我只是剛開始。</span><span lang="en">I’m only just beginning.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我這學期寫的每一行 Python，都小到不值一提。</span><span lang="en">Every line of Python I write this semester is too small to mention.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">但我知道我在往哪裡走。</span><span lang="en">But I know where I’m heading.</span></p>
