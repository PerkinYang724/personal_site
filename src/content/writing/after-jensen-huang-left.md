<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">國會要他出面說明。</span><span lang="en">Congress has asked him to come forward and explain.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他從哪裡來</span><span lang="en">Where He Came From</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">1963 年，黃仁勳出生在台南。</span><span lang="en">In 1963, Jensen Huang was born in Tainan.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">小時候住過泰國，9 歲那年，被送到美國。</span><span lang="en">He lived in Thailand as a child, and at age 9 he was sent to America.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">十幾歲的時候，他在 Denny’s 洗碗，收桌子。</span><span lang="en">In his teens, he washed dishes and cleared tables at Denny’s.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">1993 年，他和兩個朋友坐在 Denny’s 的一張桌子前，創辦了 NVIDIA。</span><span lang="en">In 1993, he and two friends sat at a table in a Denny’s and founded NVIDIA.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">公司有一度差點撐不下去。</span><span lang="en">At one point the company almost didn’t make it.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他飛到日本，請 Sega 再給一次機會，那 500 萬美元把公司從邊緣拉了回來。</span><span lang="en">He flew to Japan and asked Sega for one more chance, and that $5 million pulled the company back from the edge.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">接下來的 33 年，他從遊戲顯示卡，走到 AI 的算力。</span><span lang="en">In the 33 years that followed, he carried it from gaming graphics cards to the compute behind AI.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">台灣，和泰國</span><span lang="en">Taiwan, and Thailand</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我在美國快 8 年了。</span><span lang="en">I have been in America for almost 8 years.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">這 8 年，每次我說我來自台灣，總有人皺著眉問：泰國？</span><span lang="en">In these 8 years, every time I said I came from Taiwan, someone frowned and asked: Thailand?</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">Taiwan 和 Thailand，在很多人耳裡長得很像。</span><span lang="en">In many ears, Taiwan and Thailand look nearly alike.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">這幾年，風向轉了。</span><span lang="en">In recent years, the wind turned.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">大家開始用 AI。</span><span lang="en">Everyone started using AI.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">AI 要訓練，訓練要算力，算力要 GPU。</span><span lang="en">AI needs training, training needs compute, compute needs GPUs.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">順著這條線往下找，找到 NVIDIA，再找到台積電。</span><span lang="en">Follow that line down and you find NVIDIA, and then TSMC.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">很多人從沒翻開過一頁晶片的資料，卻因為 ChatGPT，第一次認真聽見「台灣」兩個字。</span><span lang="en">Many people have never opened a page about chips, yet because of ChatGPT they heard the word “Taiwan” seriously for the first time.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">這幾年，在 GTC，在各種場合，黃仁勳一次又一次說起台灣。</span><span lang="en">In recent years, at GTC and on other stages, Jensen Huang has spoken of Taiwan again and again.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他生在這裡，也愛這裡，聽得出來。</span><span lang="en">He was born here and loves it here, and you can hear it.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">對我這個 24 歲的留學生，他是我在美國看得見的一個標竿。</span><span lang="en">For me, a 24-year-old international student, he is a mark I can see ahead of me in America.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">每次在 LinkedIn 上讀到「Denny’s 洗碗工」那一行，我就覺得，可以再撐一下。</span><span lang="en">Every time I read the “Denny’s dishwasher” line on LinkedIn, I think: I can hold on a little longer.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">一個朋友的比喻</span><span lang="en">A Friend’s Metaphor</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我有個創業的朋友 Sean。</span><span lang="en">I have a friend, Sean, who is a founder.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">前陣子他告訴我，他想把 CEO 的位置讓出來。</span><span lang="en">Not long ago he told me he wants to give up the CEO seat.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他說，那像是把自己養大的孩子，送給別人領養。</span><span lang="en">He said it feels like handing the child you raised to someone else to adopt.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">你明明知道那是你的孩子，卻相信別人更適合帶他走接下來的路。</span><span lang="en">You know full well the child is yours, yet you believe someone else can carry him down the road ahead better.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">Sean 是自己選的。</span><span lang="en">Sean chose it himself.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">黃仁勳不一樣，外面的人到現在還在拼湊細節。</span><span lang="en">Jensen Huang is different. People outside are still piecing the details together.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">可是兩件事走到同一個地方：CEO 換人了。</span><span lang="en">Yet both roads arrive at the same place: a new CEO.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我發現，我替 Sean 難過得少，替黃仁勳難過得多。</span><span lang="en">I notice I feel less sad for Sean, and much more sad for Jensen Huang.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">因為我只能隔得很遠看他，連一句謝謝都沒機會說。</span><span lang="en">Because I could only watch him from far away, and never got the chance to say thank you.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我正在學的事</span><span lang="en">What I Am Learning</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">這個學期，我在埋地基。</span><span lang="en">This semester, I am laying a foundation.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我 vibe code 了將近兩年。</span><span lang="en">I have vibe coded for almost two years.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">把需求丟給 AI，程式就長出來，跑起來，很痛快。</span><span lang="en">I toss a request to AI, the code grows out, it runs, and it feels good.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">很多東西一次就成，以後只會更輕鬆。</span><span lang="en">Many things work on the first try, and it will only get easier.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">越來越多不是工程師的人，會這樣做事。</span><span lang="en">More and more people who are not engineers will work this way.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">可是我開始卡住了。</span><span lang="en">But I started to get stuck.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">程式報錯，我把整段錯誤訊息貼回去，請 AI 修。</span><span lang="en">When the code threw an error, I pasted the whole error message back and asked AI to fix it.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">它修好一個，又冒出一個。</span><span lang="en">It fixed one, and another appeared.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我說不出它動了哪一行，也說不出之前為什麼會壞。</span><span lang="en">I couldn’t say which line it touched, or why it had broken before.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">程式跑得動，我卻說不出它為什麼跑得動。</span><span lang="en">The code ran, and I couldn’t say why it ran.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">所以我開始學 Python，一行一行讀。</span><span lang="en">So I started learning Python, reading it one line at a time.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">很慢，有時候很挫折。</span><span lang="en">It is slow, and sometimes it hurts.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">身邊有人說，AI 都會寫了，你何必學。</span><span lang="en">People around me say: AI can write it already, why learn it?</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我懂這個說法。</span><span lang="en">I understand that argument.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我想要的是，它壞掉的時候，我知道從哪裡下手。</span><span lang="en">What I want is to know where to start when it breaks.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">地基是一層壓著一層的。</span><span lang="en">A foundation is layer pressed upon layer.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">為了快，跳過一層，那一層的問題不會消失，只會站在後面等你。</span><span lang="en">If you skip a layer to go faster, its problems do not vanish. They stand behind you and wait.</span></p>

<h2 class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我也要放手一件事</span><span lang="en">I Am Letting Go of Something Too</span></h2>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">最近，我做了一個決定。</span><span lang="en">Recently, I made a decision.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我要放下創業，把力氣放回學校，放在畢業後留在美國的那條路上。</span><span lang="en">I am putting down my startup and putting my strength back into school, and onto the road that lets me stay in America after I graduate.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">創業是我最放不下的一件事。</span><span lang="en">The startup is the thing I find hardest to let go of.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">每次想到要停，我就想起 Sean 說的，把自己養大的孩子交給別人。</span><span lang="en">Every time I think about stopping, I remember what Sean said: giving the child you raised to someone else.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我現在比以前更懂黃仁勳離開這件事。</span><span lang="en">I understand Jensen Huang’s leaving better now than before.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我們離開的原因，一點都不一樣。</span><span lang="en">Our reasons for leaving are nothing alike.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">他那邊有調查，有國會，有我看不清的角落。</span><span lang="en">On his side there are investigations, Congress, and corners I can’t see into.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我這邊，只有一張畢業時間表。</span><span lang="en">On my side, there is only a graduation timeline.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">可是那個動作，我懂了一點：把手從自己做出來的東西上，慢慢鬆開。</span><span lang="en">But I understand that motion a little now: slowly loosening your hand from the thing you made.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">你會問自己，甘心嗎。</span><span lang="en">You ask yourself: can you live with it?</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我給自己的答案是，先打地基。</span><span lang="en">The answer I gave myself is to lay the foundation first.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">課堂，Python，財務，一塊一塊補。</span><span lang="en">Classes, Python, finance, one stone at a time.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">畢業以後，我要有資格留在這裡。</span><span lang="en">After graduation, I want to have earned the right to stay here.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">以後如果我回頭做自己的東西，一隻手有熱情，另一隻手有修得好東西的本事。</span><span lang="en">If I turn back someday to make my own thing, I will hold passion in one hand and the skill to fix what breaks in the other.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">NVIDIA 的 GPU 還在轉。</span><span lang="en">NVIDIA’s GPUs are still running.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">人離開了位置，做過的事還在。</span><span lang="en">A person leaves the seat, and what they built is still there.</span></p>

<p class="bi"><span lang="zh-Hant">我放下創業，我做過的事也還在。</span><span lang="en">I put down my startup, and what I built is still there too.</span></p>
