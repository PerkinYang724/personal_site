Oshen Studio is a small creative AI studio I started as a student. I make cinematic videos and build AI automation workflows for creators. The workflows come from my own work. I run my projects on Notion, n8n, Zapier, and LLMs, and I share what actually holds up. Oshen also produces Still Human, a biweekly podcast where I talk with builders, creators, and thinkers about what AI is doing to the way we live and work. It started with one question: who are you without AI? Oshen is where I keep trying to answer it, one video, one workflow, and one conversation at a time.
