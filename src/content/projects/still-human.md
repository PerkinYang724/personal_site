It started with one question: who are you without AI? Each episode, I sit down with someone building with AI, writing about it, or living inside it. Founders who burned out moving fast. Researchers studying what AI does to attention and memory. Young creatives making things in a world where AI can make anything. We skip the hype and the tool tutorials. We talk about the cost of building fast, the people behind the work, and what gets lost along the way. Every conversation ends with the same question: as AI becomes more present in our lives and work, where do you feel being still human matters most?
